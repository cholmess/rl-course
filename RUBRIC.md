# Grading rubric

Each module is scored out of 100:

- **40%** quiz (on the course page)
- **40%** experiment: correct code, tests pass, sensible runs
- **20%** write-up: predictions, explanation of results, ablation analysis

| Grade | Score | What it means | Next step |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 90–100 | You can derive the method and implement it from a blank page, and you can predict and diagnose its failure modes. | Advance |
| A− | 85–89 | Solid command with small gaps you can close on your own. | Advance (minimum bar) |
| B | 75–84 | You understand it, but need references to implement it or miss key failure modes. | Remedial set, then retake |
| C | 65–74 | Concepts are shaky and the experiment works only partly. | Re-read flagged sections, redo the experiment |
| F | < 65 | Core ideas are missing. | Redo the module, possibly after a refresher |

Every quiz includes a few review questions from earlier modules. Missing those triggers a short review set, not a retake.

The predictions in your write-up are graded on whether you made a clear, testable prediction and reasoned about it afterwards, not on whether it was right.
